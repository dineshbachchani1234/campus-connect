# Design evolution log

This log feeds the "Design evolution based on feedback" section of the T4 report (16 points). Add an entry whenever a change comes from feedback: in-class low fidelity testing, our own high fidelity test sessions, or professor and TA comments.

For each entry, save a before image (photo of the paper screen or a screenshot of the earlier web version) and an after screenshot in `docs/screenshots/before-after/`, named `<number>-before.png` and `<number>-after.png`.
